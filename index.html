01 / ROLE

Practical hospitality work with real responsibility.

You’ll help keep accommodation areas clean, safe and ready for guests while working closely with onsite staff and remote reception.

01 THE WORK

What you’ll do

  • Clean and service guest rooms, pods, bathrooms and common accommodation areas.
  • Make beds, replenish linen and housekeeping supplies.
  • Work from assigned task lists and update completed duties.
  • Coordinate with onsite staff and remote reception when needed.
  • Report maintenance, damage and safety issues promptly.
02 YOU

What we’re looking for

  • Previous housekeeping experience preferred.
  • Strong attention to detail and observation skills.
  • Reliable, punctual and comfortable following structured task lists.
  • Clear communication and a team-first attitude.
  • Flexible availability including weekends and public holidays.
03 REQUIREMENTS

Before you apply

  • Valid Australian work rights are required.
  • This role is TFN employment only.
  • Applicants must be comfortable working in person around Sydney CBD.
  • Physical ability to perform regular housekeeping duties is required.
